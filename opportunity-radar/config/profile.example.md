# Personal profile — template

This committed file is a **redacted template**. The real profile is private: it
lives in `config/profile.local.md` (git-ignored) for local runs, and in the
`RADAR_PROFILE` GitHub Secret for CI.

If the scorer falls back to this file, fit scores are meaningless and the run
will say so in its log. `radar status` reports which profile is in use.

Fill in the sections below and save the result as `config/profile.local.md`.

## Who

- Name, city/region, country.
- Employment status and the hours genuinely available for something new.

## Capital

- Regular investment amount and cadence.
- Spare cash per month.
- The dollar level above which an opportunity counts as capital-heavy, and the
  level above which it is out of reach.

## Investing stance

- Style (value, growth, index, income) and what evidence moves you.
- Research watchlist tickers.
- Advice boundary: research leads only, risks stated.

## Existing ventures and assets

- Any business, audience, property or capability that an opportunity could plug
  into. These are the highest-leverage inputs to fit scoring — be specific about
  what already exists, because that is what makes a trend cheap to test.

## Skills

- Technical and non-technical, with an honest level for each.

## Scoring instructions

- State explicitly whether opportunities may fall outside these skills.
- What should raise personal fit.
- What should lower personal fit.
- The maximum size of a weekly action.
