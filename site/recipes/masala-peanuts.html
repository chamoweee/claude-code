Crunchy Masala Peanuts

The teatime snack that disappears fastest. Baking instead of deep-frying makes them keep for a week without going soft.

20min cook
6servings
mediumspice

What replaces the onion & garlic here: Chaat masala and amchur bring the tang; no onion or garlic powder anywhere.

Method

Cook's notes
