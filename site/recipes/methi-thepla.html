Methi Thepla

Gujarati thepla is the great travelling bread: it stays soft for two days unrefrigerated, and the traditional recipe has never included onion or garlic.

VeganJain
20min prep
20min cook
4servings
mediumspice
🫓
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Fresh fenugreek leaves and ajwain provide a savory-bitter depth no allium could match.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Thinner rolling gives softer thepla. Thick ones go leathery when cool.
  • Squeeze bitter methi with a little salt for 10 minutes first if you prefer it mild.
