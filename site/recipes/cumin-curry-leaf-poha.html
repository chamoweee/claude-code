Cumin & Curry Leaf Poha

A fifteen-minute breakfast that's naturally allium-free in its simplest form. The trick is rinsing the poha briefly — soak it and you get porridge.

VeganGFJain
10min prep
10min cook
2servings
mildspice
🫓
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Mustard seeds, curry leaves, and peanuts build all the savoriness; lemon does the brightening onion might.

Ingredients

2 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Turmeric goes into the oil, not onto the poha — it coats more evenly.
  • Sugar isn't optional; it balances the lemon.
