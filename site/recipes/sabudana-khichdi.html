Sabudana Khichdi

A fasting-day staple, which is precisely why it contains no onion or garlic — fasting foods in Indian tradition exclude both by definition.

VeganGF
15min prep
15min cook
3servings
mildspice
⚪
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Roasted peanut powder gives richness and body; cumin and chili do the seasoning.

Ingredients

3 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Overcooking turns sago into glue — stop as soon as the pearls go clear.
  • The peanut coating is what keeps them from clumping. Don't skip it.
