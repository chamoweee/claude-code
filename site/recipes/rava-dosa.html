Crispy Rava Dosa

The fastest dosa there is: no grinding, no overnight ferment. The batter must be shockingly thin, which is what creates the lace.

VeganJain
20min prep
20min cook
4servings
mildspice
🥞
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Cumin, pepper, and curry leaves season the batter in place of the usual chopped onion.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Whisk before every single dosa — the batter separates in under a minute.
  • If your dosa comes out solid instead of lacy, the batter is too thick. Add water.
