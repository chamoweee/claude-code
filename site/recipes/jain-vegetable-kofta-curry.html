Jain Vegetable Kofta Curry

A celebration dish built entirely without onion, garlic, or root vegetables — proof that Jain cooking can be as rich as any restaurant curry.

GFJain
20min prep
30min cook
4servings
mildspice
🥗
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Soaked cashews give the body onion paste usually provides; ginger and garam masala carry the aroma.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Squeeze the gourd hard — wet batter means koftas that fall apart in the oil.
  • Drop the koftas in at the table if you're making the gravy ahead.
