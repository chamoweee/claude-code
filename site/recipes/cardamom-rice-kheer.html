Cardamom Rice Kheer

There is no shortcut. Kheer is milk, rice, and patience — forty minutes of gentle stirring turns three ingredients into something remarkable.

GFJain
5min prep
45min cook
6servings
sweetspice
🍮
What replaces the onion & garlic here: A naturally allium-free dish, as all Indian sweets are.

Ingredients

6 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Use a heavy-bottomed pan and stir the bottom often, or it will catch and taste scorched.
  • It thickens a lot as it cools — pull it off looser than you want it.
