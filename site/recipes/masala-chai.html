Proper Masala Chai

Real chai is boiled, not steeped, and the spices go in whole and crushed fresh. Ginger and cardamom are the non-negotiables.

Method

Cook's notes

  • Crush the ginger with the flat of a knife rather than grating; you want its juice, not its fibre.
  • Boiling three times is genuinely the technique — don't shortcut it.
