Vegetable Rava Upma

Upma gets a bad reputation only because it's usually made badly. Toast the semolina properly and use exactly 1:2.5 semolina to water and it comes out light every time.

VeganJain
10min prep
15min cook
3servings
mildspice
🍚
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Ginger, green chili, and curry leaves provide the aromatics — no onion needed for a savory base.

Ingredients

3 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Toasting is the whole game. Untoasted semolina makes a gluey upma.
  • Add a spoon of ghee at the end for the glossy restaurant finish.
