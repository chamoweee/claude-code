Tomato & Ginger Chutney

Cook tomatoes long enough and they develop the same savory depth people chase with onion. This keeps a week in the fridge.

VeganGFJain
10min prep
20min cook
6servings
mediumspice
🍅
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Slow-cooked tomato plus a heavy hand with ginger replaces the usual onion-garlic base completely.

Ingredients

6 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Don't rush the reduction — this is where the savory depth comes from.
  • Excellent with dosa, thepla, or simply on toast.
