Hing-Tempered Dal Tadka

The dish that convinces people no-allium cooking loses nothing. All the savory depth here comes from asafoetida bloomed in hot ghee — it turns nutty and garlicky the moment it hits the fat.

GFJain
10min prep
25min cook
4servings
mediumspice
🍛
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Asafoetida (hing) does the work garlic normally would; ginger and tomato supply the sweetness you'd get from onion.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Add the asafoetida off-heat for a second if your pan runs hot — it turns bitter if it scorches.
  • A squeeze of lemon at the table lifts the whole bowl.
