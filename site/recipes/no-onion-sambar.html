No-Onion Sambar

Traditional temple sambar has never used onion. The sourness of tamarind and the aroma of a curry-leaf tempering are the whole point.

VeganGF
15min prep
30min cook
6servings
mediumspice
🥣
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Tamarind and sambar powder define the flavor; a curry-leaf and mustard tempering replaces any aromatics.

Ingredients

6 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Add okra separately at the end so it doesn't turn slimy.
  • The tempering is not optional — it's where 80% of the flavor lives.
