Gajar Halwa (Carrot Pudding)

Winter's great dessert. The transformation from raw grated carrot to deep orange halwa takes about an hour of reduction, and there's no substituting for it.

GF
20min prep
45min cook
6servings
sweetspice
🥕
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Naturally allium-free — as all traditional Indian sweets are.

Ingredients

6 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Grate the carrots coarsely; fine grating turns to mush.
  • The moment the ghee starts separating at the edges, it's done.
