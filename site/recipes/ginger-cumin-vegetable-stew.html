Ginger-Cumin Vegetable Stew

The weeknight dish to make when you want something warming and undemanding. Everything goes in one pot and it looks after itself.

VeganGF
15min prep
25min cook
4servings
mildspice
🍲
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Toasted whole coriander and a generous amount of ginger build the savory base.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Add quick-cooking vegetables like peas in the last 5 minutes only.
  • Toasting and crushing the coriander yourself is a noticeable upgrade over pre-ground.
