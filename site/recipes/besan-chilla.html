Besan Chilla (Chickpea Pancakes)

High-protein, naturally gluten-free, and ready in twenty minutes. Chilla is the no-allium cook's answer to an omelette.

VeganGFJain
10min prep
15min cook
3servings
mediumspice
🥙
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Ajwain (carom seed) and ginger give the batter its savory bite where onion is usually grated in.

Ingredients

3 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Rest the batter — it hydrates the flour and stops a raw, chalky taste.
  • Medium heat only; high heat browns the outside before the inside sets.
