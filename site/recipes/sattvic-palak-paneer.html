Sattvic Palak Paneer

The secret to green — not khaki — palak paneer is blanching the spinach for exactly two minutes and shocking it in ice water before blending.

GFJain
10min prep
20min cook
4servings
mildspice
🥬
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Ginger and green chili carry the heat that garlic usually would; hing adds the savory bottom note.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Never cover the pan after the spinach goes in; trapped steam turns it olive-drab.
  • Pan-sear the paneer cubes first if you like a firmer bite.
