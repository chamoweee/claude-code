Lemon Rice (Chitranna)

The great South Indian lunchbox dish, and the best possible use for yesterday's rice. Naturally free of onion and garlic in every traditional version.

VeganGFJain
10min prep
10min cook
4servings
mildspice
🍋
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Toasted dals and peanuts add crunch and savor; lemon supplies all the brightness.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Cold, day-old rice works far better than fresh, which turns to mush.
  • Adding lemon off the heat keeps it bright instead of bitter.
