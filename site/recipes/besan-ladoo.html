Besan Ladoo

The entire recipe is besan, ghee, sugar, and cardamom. All the flavor comes from roasting the flour slowly until it turns the color of wet sand and smells nutty.

GFJain
10min prep
25min cook
12servings
sweetspice
🟡
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Naturally allium-free; a temple-prasad classic.

Ingredients

12 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • If the mix won't hold together, add a spoon of warm melted ghee.
  • Coarse besan gives the classic grainy texture; fine flour makes them dense.
