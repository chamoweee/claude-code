Fresh Coconut Chutney

Every South Indian breakfast needs this. The classic recipe contains no allium whatsoever, just coconut, chili, and a hot tempering poured over the top.

VeganGFJain
10min prep
5min cook
4servings
mildspice
🥥
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Roasted gram dal thickens and adds nuttiness; ginger provides the bite.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Roasted gram dal is what keeps the chutney from separating. Cashews work in a pinch.
  • Make it the same day — fresh coconut sours quickly.
