No-Onion Rajma (Kidney Bean Curry)

The creaminess in good rajma comes from beans breaking down, not from onion. Cook them long enough and the gravy thickens itself.

VeganGFJain
10min prep
45min cook
4servings
mediumspice
🍲
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Mashed beans supply the body; hing and ginger supply the savory aroma.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Undercooked kidney beans are both unpleasant and unsafe — cook them fully soft.
  • The gravy should coat a spoon; if it's thin, simmer longer uncovered.
