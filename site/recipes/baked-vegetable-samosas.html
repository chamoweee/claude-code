Baked Vegetable Samosas

Classic samosa filling was never about onion — it's the amchur and roasted cumin that make it taste like a samosa.

Vegan
25min prep
25min cook
6servings
mediumspice
🥟
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Amchur, roasted cumin, and ginger define the filling; no onion required.

Ingredients

6 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Cool the filling fully or the pastry goes soggy and tears.
  • For a Jain version, swap the potato for crumbled paneer or green banana.
