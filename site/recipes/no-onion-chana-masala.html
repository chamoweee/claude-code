No-Onion Chana Masala

Chana masala is one of the easiest curries to make allium-free because its signature flavor was never onion — it's the sour-smoky combination of amchur, anardana, and toasted cumin.

VeganGFJain
10min prep
35min cook
4servings
mediumspice
🫘
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Dried mango powder and pomegranate seed do the heavy lifting; no onion base needed at all.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • A black tea bag dropped in while the chickpeas simmer gives the classic dark color.
  • This tastes better on day two — make it ahead.
