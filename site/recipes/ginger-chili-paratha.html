Ginger & Green Chili Paratha

A stuffed paratha that gets its punch from fresh ginger and chili rather than the onion most street versions use.

VeganJain
20min prep
20min cook
4servings
mediumspice
🥖
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Raw ginger and green chili give heat and aroma; asafoetida rounds it out.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • The spiral fold gives the most layers — roll it like a cinnamon bun, then flatten.
  • Rest the dough properly; unrested dough springs back and tears.
