No-Onion Paneer Butter Masala

Most butter masala leans on onion paste for sweetness. This version gets there with slow-cooked tomato, soaked cashews, and a pinch of sugar, which is how many temple kitchens have always made it.

GFJain
15min prep
30min cook
4servings
mildspice
🧀
What replaces the onion & garlic here: Long-cooked tomato plus cashew replaces the onion base entirely; asafoetida stands in for garlic.

Ingredients

4 servings

Method

Cook's notes

  • Soak the paneer in hot salted water for 10 minutes first; it stays soft in the gravy.
  • Straining the puree is the difference between homestyle and restaurant texture.
