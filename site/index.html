Why cook this way

Flavorful food, gentler on the body and the spirit

Skipping onion and garlic doesn't mean skipping flavor. We lean on ginger, asafoetida, warm spices, and slow cooking to build deep, satisfying dishes.

🙏

Sattvic & Jain-friendly

Rooted in Ayurvedic and Jain culinary traditions, where alliums are considered too stimulating for a calm, clear mind.

🌱

Gentle on digestion

Many people find onion and garlic (high-FODMAP foods) trigger bloating — our recipes are naturally easier to digest.

🔥

Still bold & aromatic

Asafoetida (hing), ginger, curry leaves, and toasted spices deliver the same savory depth without a single allium.
