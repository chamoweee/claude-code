Why cook this way

Flavorful food, gentler on the body and the spirit

Skipping onion and garlic doesn't mean skipping flavor. We lean on ginger, asafoetida, warm spices, and slow cooking to build deep, satisfying dishes.

🙏

Sattvic & Jain-friendly

Rooted in Ayurvedic and Jain culinary traditions, where alliums are considered too stimulating for a calm, clear mind.

🌱

Gentle on digestion

Many people find onion and garlic (high-FODMAP foods) trigger bloating — our recipes are naturally easier to digest.

🔥

Still bold & aromatic

Asafoetida (hing), ginger, curry leaves, and toasted spices deliver the same savory depth without a single allium.

The swap guide

What we reach for instead

Three techniques replace almost everything onion and garlic were doing.

🌾

Asafoetida for garlic

A pinch of hing bloomed in hot ghee turns savory and pungent in seconds — the single most important substitution in this kitchen.

🫚

Ginger for bite

Fresh ginger and green chili bring the heat and brightness raw garlic normally would, without any of the harshness.

🍅

Slow tomato for body

Cooked down until the oil separates, tomato builds the sweet, savory base that onion paste usually provides.
