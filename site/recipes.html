Hing-Tempered Dal Tadka

⏱ 35 min🍽 Serves 4

Creamy yellow lentils finished with a sizzling asafoetida and cumin tempering.

Ingredients & steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup split yellow lentils (toor dal)
  • 1 tsp ghee or oil
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • Pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric, chili powder to taste
  • Fresh coriander, salt

Steps

  1. Pressure-cook lentils with turmeric and salt until soft.
  2. Heat ghee, add cumin seeds and asafoetida until fragrant.
  3. Add ginger and tomato; cook until soft.
  4. Stir the tempering into the dal, simmer 5 minutes, and top with coriander.

Jain Vegetable Kofta Curry

⏱ 50 min🍽 Serves 4

Root-vegetable dumplings simmered in a creamy tomato-cashew gravy.

Ingredients & steps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups grated raw banana & bottle gourd (no potato)
  • 2 tbsp chickpea flour
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 10 cashews, soaked and blended
  • 1 tsp ginger paste, 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Oil for shallow-frying, salt, cilantro

Steps

  1. Mix grated vegetables with chickpea flour and salt; shape into small balls and shallow-fry until golden.
  2. Simmer tomato puree with cashew paste, ginger, and garam masala for 10 minutes.
  3. Add koftas just before serving so they stay tender; garnish with cilantro.

Ginger-Cumin Vegetable Stew

⏱ 40 min🍽 Serves 4

A slow-simmered one-pot stew built on ginger, cumin, and toasted coriander.

Ingredients & steps

Ingredients

  • 3 cups mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, beans, squash)
  • 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp ground coriander, 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • Salt, lemon juice, cilantro

Steps

  1. Toast cumin seeds in oil, add ginger and ground spices.
  2. Add vegetables and broth; simmer covered until tender, about 25 minutes.
  3. Finish with lemon juice and cilantro.

Cumin-Curry Leaf Vegetable Poha

⏱ 20 min🍽 Serves 2

Flattened rice tossed with mustard seeds, curry leaves, peanuts, and peas.

Ingredients & steps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups thick poha (flattened rice), rinsed
  • 1 tbsp oil, 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 8-10 curry leaves
  • 2 tbsp peanuts
  • 1/2 cup peas, 1 tsp turmeric
  • Lemon juice, salt, cilantro

Steps

  1. Heat oil, splutter mustard seeds and curry leaves, then toast peanuts.
  2. Add peas and turmeric, cook briefly, then fold in the soaked poha.
  3. Season with salt and lemon juice; garnish with cilantro.

Baked Vegetable Samosas

⏱ 45 min🍽 Makes 12

Crisp baked pastry filled with spiced peas, carrots, and potato — no onion filling here.

Ingredients & steps

Ingredients

  • 12 samosa pastry sheets or wrappers
  • 2 potatoes, boiled and cubed
  • 1/2 cup peas, 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 green chili, chopped
  • 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp amchur (dry mango powder)
  • Oil, salt

Steps

  1. Sauté ginger and chili in oil, add carrots and peas until tender.
  2. Fold in potatoes and spices; cool the filling.
  3. Fill and fold pastry into samosas; brush with oil and bake at 400°F (200°C) for 22-25 minutes until golden.

Jeera Vegetable Pulao

⏱ 30 min🍽 Serves 4

Fragrant basmati rice simmered with whole spices, cumin, and garden vegetables.

Ingredients & steps

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups basmati rice, rinsed
  • 1 tbsp ghee, 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 bay leaf, 2 cloves, 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (beans, carrot, peas)
  • 2.5 cups water, salt

Steps

  1. Toast whole spices in ghee until fragrant.
  2. Add vegetables and rice; stir to coat.
  3. Add water and salt, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer 15 minutes until rice is fluffy.

Cardamom Rice Kheer

⏱ 45 min🍽 Serves 6

A slow-simmered milk rice pudding scented with cardamom, saffron, and pistachio.

Ingredients & steps

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup basmati rice
  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 4 green cardamom pods, crushed
  • Pinch of saffron, chopped pistachios

Steps

  1. Simmer rice in milk over low heat, stirring often, until thickened (about 30 minutes).
  2. Stir in sugar, cardamom, and saffron; cook 5 more minutes.
  3. Chill or serve warm, topped with pistachios.

Chickpea Flour Vegetable Uttapam

⏱ 25 min🍽 Serves 3

Savory chickpea-flour pancakes studded with grated carrot, cabbage, and cilantro.

Ingredients & steps

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups chickpea flour (besan)
  • Water to make a thick batter
  • 1/2 cup grated carrot & cabbage
  • 1 green chili, chopped, 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • Salt, oil for cooking

Steps

  1. Whisk chickpea flour with water into a smooth, thick batter; season with salt and cumin.
  2. Stir in vegetables and chili.
  3. Pour onto a hot, oiled griddle and cook both sides until golden and crisp at the edges.
