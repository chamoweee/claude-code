About us

A bakery, not a warehouse

We started in 2019 with one deck oven and a stubborn view about what a sausage roll should taste like.

How we got here

🌾

Everything from scratch

We laminate our own puff over three days, braise our own beef, and keep a sourdough starter that predates the shop. Nothing arrives pre-made.

👋

Small team, long hours

Six bakers, in at 2am. You'll usually be served by the person who made what you're buying, which keeps everyone honest.

📍

Part of the neighbourhood

We bake for the local schools, the business parks and a good number of birthdays. If you live nearby, we've probably made your kid's cake.

Come and see

The ovens run from 2am and the cabinet is full by six.
